Services

Advisory built around where your company is right now and where it needs to go.

Engagements are scoped to the objectives of your company. Below is a summary of our standard services. 

Fractional CFO

Joins the executive team to oversee the finance function — bookkeeping, controller, and FP&A — building lasting systems and protocols while the company grows into the need for a full-time CFO.

The work is set by the CEO's actual goal, whether that's growing the business, maintaining market share, moving toward profitability, or simply freeing CEO mindshare from the finance function.

  • Oversight of bookkeeping, controller, and FP&A functions
  • Financial systems and protocols built to scale with the business
  • Direct partnership with the CEO on strategic priorities

Capital Strategy Advisory

Advises companies preparing to raise on refining the pitch, building the model, populating the data room, and understanding capital needs and timing ahead of investor dialogue.

Works with the executive team to weigh the tradeoffs between dilutive and non-dilutive capital, looking beyond the current round toward building a holistic, long-term strategy.

  • Pitch, model, and data room preparation
  • Capital needs and timing analysis
  • Dilutive vs. non-dilutive capital tradeoffs

About

Adam Erickson

Adam has built a career out of leaning into hard problems on unfamiliar ground. As a Marine intelligence officer leading a team in Afghanistan, the job was to assess a high-stakes, fast-moving situation from nebulous information — asking the right questions, fusing scattered intelligence into a clear picture, then building the team and process to act on it.

That same instinct carried onto Wall Street, where structuring and negotiating transactions at Barclays and BlackRock demanded the same attention to detail and comfort operating without a clear picture — reading a deal, a market, or a management team correctly, then assembling the high-impact teams needed to get it done.

Most recently, that meant becoming an operator himself — buying an established business and professionalizing its finance function from the inside, trading the advisor's chair for the owner's.

At Tall Tree Associates, we bring that combination — owner-operator perspective and institutional-grade discipline — to help our partners and clients meet their next objective.
